About The Project

Bridging the gap between recipe discovery and community feedback for home cooks

Fork & Pen transforms traditional one-way cooking blogs into a collaborative culinary community, empowering cooks to exchange verified recipe techniques, upload finished dishes, and curate custom collections on ASP.NET.

What It Does

Key Features

Four core pillars that inspire everyday cooking and collaborative recipe creation

✦ Pick a culinary station to explore a feature

01Creation

Recipe management

Upload detailed recipes complete with ingredient ratios, step-by-step directions, cooking times, and automated 10MB photo-resizing for instant web optimization.

  • Ingredient scaling
  • 10MB auto-resize
  • Step-by-step steps
02Community

Social networking

A built-in friend system connects culinary enthusiasts, allowing users to follow home cooks, share activity feeds, and tailor recommendations by diet.

  • Cook friend system
  • Dietary preferences
  • Personal profiles
03Validation

"I had made this" reviews

Go beyond star ratings: community cooks upload photos of their completed dishes with honest cooking notes, verifying that recipes truly work at home.

  • "I Had Made This"
  • Dish photo uploads
  • Star ratings & tips
04Organization

Custom collections

Organize saved recipes into personalized, themed recipe binders (e.g., Weeknight Dinners, Holiday Baking) and share them seamlessly with friends.

  • Themed binders
  • One-click saving
  • Social recipe sharing
