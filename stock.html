About The Project

Eliminating manual paperwork and inventory shortages for Kluang Sarawak Kolo Mee

StockTrack replaces error-prone daily paper logbooks with real-time stock monitoring, automated usage logs, and instant shortage alerts to prevent kitchen service disruptions and minimize food waste.

What It Does

Key Features

Four core modules that keep ingredients stocked, tracked, and cost-controlled

✦ Pick an inventory module to explore a feature

01Monitoring

Real-time alerts

Continuously tracks inventory thresholds and flags low-stock ingredients with an instant red highlight to prevent dining service disruptions.

  • Live stock levels
  • Visual threshold alerts
  • Zero kitchen downtime
02Operations

Stock entry & withdrawal

Automates daily kitchen intake and ingredient withdrawal logging with instant balance adjustments, replacing handwritten paper sheets.

  • One-click deductions
  • Purchase logging
  • Usage verification
03Analytics

Log & reporting module

Maintains comprehensive historical logs of every stock transaction, generating analytical reports to evaluate food costs and eliminate waste.

  • Audit trails
  • Analytical reports
  • Waste prevention
04Governance

Suppliers & role access

Digitally organizes supplier contacts for streamlined purchasing, with role-based authentication restricting administrative changes to authorized managers.

  • Supplier directory
  • Role permissions
  • Secure session control
