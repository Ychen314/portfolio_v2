About The Project

Solving daily campus transportation challenges with trusted student ride sharing

SecureRide is a carpooling mobile app designed around the transport problems UTHM students face every day. It works as one central platform that matches students who have vehicles with students who need a ride.

What It Does

Key Features

Four core pillars that make rides safer, cheaper, and easier to arrange

✦ Pick a passenger to explore a feature

Verified community

Students must verify their identity with the official UTHM email domain, so only legitimate students can use the platform. Unverified accounts cannot book or offer rides, ensuring everyone in the car is an authentic campus peer.

Live GPS tracking

Passengers can see the driver's live location and share their trip progress with authorized trusted contacts. Real-time updates give friends and family peace of mind from pickup until safe drop-off on or off campus.

Ride matching

Drivers post ride offers with their route, departure time, and available seats. Passengers can browse matching trips, reserve a seat securely, and split petrol costs seamlessly without messy chat groups.

Web admin portal

Administrators get a comprehensive dashboard to oversee student user accounts, verify university credentials, review trip logs, and monitor campus carpooling safety in real time.
