Overview

About The Project

Solving daily campus transportation challenges with trusted student ride sharing

SecureRide is one central app that matches students who have a vehicle with students who need a ride, built around the transport problems UTHM students face every day.

High e-hailing costs
Limited campus parking
Uncertified ride groups
What It Does

Key Features

Four core pillars that make rides safer, cheaper, and easier to arrange

✦ Pick a passenger to explore a feature

01Trust

Verified community

Only students with an official UTHM email can book or offer rides, so everyone in the car is a real campus peer.

  • UTHM email
  • Verified accounts
  • Firebase Auth
02Safety

Live GPS tracking

Passengers follow the driver's live location and share trip progress with trusted contacts until safe drop-off.

  • Live location
  • Trip sharing
  • Google Cloud APIs
03Matching

Ride matching

Drivers post their route, time and free seats. Passengers browse matching trips and reserve a seat in a few taps.

  • Route
  • Seat booking
  • Cost sharing
04Management

Web admin portal

A dashboard for administrators to manage accounts, check credentials and review trips across the campus network.

  • Accounts
  • Verification
  • Trip logs
