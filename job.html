About The Project

Modernizing talent acquisition with unified dashboards and automated candidate workflows

VibeHire unifies job discovery and recruiter operations into one seamless hiring portal, equipped with automated PDF resume generation, real-time pipeline tracking, and in-app direct communication.

What It Does

Key Features

Four core pillars that accelerate hiring and empower modern talent

✦ Pick a recruitment module to explore a feature

01Workflows

Role-based dashboards

Dedicated portals tailored for both parties: Employers track job openings and candidate pipelines, while Job Seekers monitor live applications and bookmarks.

  • Employer dashboard
  • Candidate portal
  • Role authentication
02Generation

Automated resume builder

A built-in wizard that compiles candidate education, skills, and experience directly into a clean, standardized PDF formatted via DomPDF ready for instant download.

  • DomPDF generation
  • Standardized layout
  • Instant PDF export
03Visibility

Application tracking

Eliminates application black holes: candidates follow real-time status updates (Pending, Accepted, Rejected), while preventing accidental duplicate submissions.

  • Real-time status
  • Duplicate prevention
  • Recruiter feedback
04Networking

Community feed & messaging

Fosters authentic professional relationships with an industry feed for multimedia posts, coupled with secure one-to-one candidate/recruiter direct messaging.

  • Community feed
  • Direct chat
  • Read receipts
